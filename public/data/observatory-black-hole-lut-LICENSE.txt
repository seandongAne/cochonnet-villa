Schwarzschild ray lookup tables
================================

The following files are unmodified outputs of the preprocessing program in
Eric Bruneton's black_hole_shader project:

  observatory-black-hole-ray-deflection-v1.bin
  observatory-black-hole-ray-inverse-radius-v1.bin

The lookup coordinate transforms and constant-time TraceRay procedure adapted
in src/villa-map/observatory-relativistic-lens.js use the same licence below.
