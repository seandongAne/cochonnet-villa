Modified for Cochonnet Villa: the central vertical column of the model's
black_hole_light1 emissive texture is transposed into a 512x1 colour lookup.
The runtime maps part of that strip onto its dynamic Kerr accretion disc.
Original mesh geometry is not redistributed. This attribution does not imply
endorsement by the original author. Source hashes and regeneration instructions
are in src/villa-map/observatory-disc-palette-data.js and
scripts/extract-black-hole-palette.mjs.
