Observatory Kerr transfer atlas data
====================================

Files covered after they are generated by
scripts/build-kerr-transfer-atlas.mjs:

  observatory-kerr-sky-v1.bin
  observatory-kerr-disc-primary-v1.bin
  observatory-kerr-disc-secondary-v1.bin
  observatory-kerr-path-v1.bin
  observatory-kerr-transfer-atlas-v1.meta.json

The binary values are original numerical output from the generator in this
repository. No source code or precomputed data from AART is copied or
redistributed. The underlying Kerr geodesic equations are physical facts; the
primary scientific references and exact equations are recorded in the metadata.
