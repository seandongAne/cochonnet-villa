Paint a single ordinary, friendly little pig who has just come back to the villa with his finds. His distinctive pose: he tilts his head with a quietly pleased smile and lifts the bag slightly with a small cloven hoof, so the two used, transparent, slightly squashed PET water bottles peeking out of the open yellow canvas satchel are easy to see. Show the strap across his body and the solid red scarf separately. For a gallery-size thumbnail compose a close, square waist-up portrait: his broad round face fills the upper half, both ears in frame, satchel and its opening comfortably inside the lower part of the image. Soft asymmetrical folded pig ears with short wisps catching the light; big expressive amber-brown eyes with a visible iris, soft eye white and painted catchlights, rosy full cheeks, rounded coral snout with volume. Rich hand-painted storybook portrait just like image 1: fine warm ink contours, layered brushwork, deep ochre shadows, luminous warm sunlight touching the ears from the side. Background edge-to-edge: a softly out-of-focus, leafy villa garden path and wooden fence in late afternoon. Keep the down-to-earth guest distinct from the models: no checks on his scarf, no mud markings, apron, bandana, hats, jewellery, or cleaning tools. Bottles should read as ordinary discarded disposable plastic, with short threaded necks and dents, no brand or label. No other characters, letters or watermark. No beige paper backdrop, distant full-body framing or bead eyes.
