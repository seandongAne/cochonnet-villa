chimney and a small central oculus. A smaller gabled entrance canopy establishes

## Mushroom exterior

rosehip cap replaces the overlapping spheres and cylinders. Ivory freckles
follow its surface, and tapered curved gills follow its underside. The plaster
stem has a pinched profile, rounded entrance mass, sage arched door, round honey
windows, shutters, lanterns and small foundation planting. The underground
three-floor interior and observatory are unchanged.
